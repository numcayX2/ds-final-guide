Slide 22 outlines a decision framework titled “ใช้ ปรับ หรือยังไม่ใช่: ข้อเสนอต้องตามหลักฐาน” (Use, Adjust, or Not Yet Use: The Proposal Must Be Supported by Evidence). It establishes that every option—whether to use, adjust, or not yet implement a proposal—must be justified with evidence and include risk assessment and mitigation strategies.

Key criteria for each category are detailed as follows:

**Use (✅ ใช้):**
- Clear benefits and alignment with objectives.
- Positive impact on users at an acceptable level.
- Continuous monitoring plan in place.

**Adjust (⚙️ ปรับ):**
- Limited scope of application (e.g., specific target groups/low-risk areas).
- Additional data collection required (e.g., validation, further testing).
- Defined outcomes and limitations for deployment.

**Not Yet Use (❌ ยังไม่ใช่):**
- Insufficient performance or high user risk.
- Potential negative consequences including legal, ethical, or privacy concerns.
- Requires additional evidence or technology readiness before implementation.

The slide concludes with a guiding principle: decisions must be based on solid evidence, considering risks, benefits, and constraints.
