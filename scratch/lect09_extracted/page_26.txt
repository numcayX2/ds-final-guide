Health-risk Algorithm: Costs do not equal care needs.

Proxy selection error causes models to target non-standard goals.

Target: Care needs (sick patients requiring care).
Proxy Selected: Health costs (costs incurred in past/present).

Model Bias Example (Proxy / Label Bias):
- White Patient Group: Risk Score 0.8, Cost ≈ 12,000 Baht/year. Conditions: High blood pressure.
- Black Patient Group: Risk Score 0.8, Cost ≈ 12,000 Baht/year. Conditions: High blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease.

Research Findings:
- Scores are identical.
- Black patients have higher morbidity than white patients despite the same proxy score.
- Correction Results: The percentage of black patients receiving assistance increased from 17.7% to 46.5%.

Solution Direction:
- Select Target and Proxy so they align with actual desired outcomes.

Key Takeaway:
- Using a Proxy accurately does not imply that resource allocation is normal/fair.

Source: Obermeyer et al., Science, 2019.
