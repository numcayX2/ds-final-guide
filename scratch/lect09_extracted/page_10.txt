### Slide 10: Historical Bias in Data Science Models

**Core Concept:**
The slide illustrates how historical data can introduce bias into machine learning models. This occurs when the training data does not represent a fair or random distribution of outcomes across different groups, leading the model to learn incorrect patterns.

**Key Takeaway:**
Historical data is often not neutral. If opportunities were not distributed equally in the past (e.g., some customer groups received more attention than others), the model will learn these biases instead of true underlying patterns.
