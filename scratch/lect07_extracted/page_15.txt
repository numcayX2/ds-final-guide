The image presents the anatomy of a Decision Tree, detailing its core components:

- **Root Node**: The initial point where all Training Observations enter the tree.
- **Internal Node**: A node that applies a Learned Split Rule to divide observations based on features and thresholds.
- **Branch**: The path resulting from the application of a condition.
- **Class Distribution**: The count or proportion of Target Classes within a specific node.
- **Depth**: The number of split levels along a Root-to-Leaf Path.
- **Takeaway**: Understanding each part is essential for reading a Decision Tree.
