**Validation Evidence Selects Tree Complexity**

**Model Development Roles:**
*   **Training Data:** Used to learn Candidate Trees at different depths.
*   **Validation Data:** Used to compare `max_depth` values.

**Candidate Comparison:**
*   **max_depth = 1:** Validation result: low
*   **max_depth = 3:** Validation result: high (Selected for Development)
*   **max_depth = 6:** Validation result: lower
*   **unrestricted:** Validation result: materially lower

**Protect Final Evidence:**
*   **Final Test Set:** Remains locked and unused during the selection process. It is only used after modeling choices are finalized.

**Takeaway:**
*   Validation drives model choices, while Test Evidence must be protected until the final stage.
