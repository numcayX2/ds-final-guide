2. **Precision**:

   - Calculation: \( \text{Precision} = \frac{4}{4 + 1} = 0.80 \)
   - Interpretation: When the model predicts "play," it is correct 80% of the time.

3. **F-measure (F1-score)**:
   - Formula: \( \text{F1} = \frac{2 \times \text{TP}}{2 \times \text{TP} + \text{FP} + \text{FN}} \)
   - Calculation: \( \text{F1} = \frac{2 \times 4}{2 \times 4 + 1 + 2} = 0.73 \)
   - Interpretation: F1-score balances Precision and Recall, providing a single metric that considers both false positives and false negatives.

4. **Accuracy**:
   - Formula: \( \text{Accuracy} = \frac{\text{TP} + \text{TN}}{\text{Total Predictions}} \)
   - Calculation: \( \text{Accuracy} = \frac{4 + 3}{10} = 0.70 \)
   - Interpretation: The model accurately predicts 70% of the total instances.
