**Homework #2: Select a Classifier Model for Customer Inactivity, where the chosen Metric aligns with Retention Decision outcomes.**

*   **Evaluation Context:** Same Evaluation Set of 100 Customers.
*   **Positive Class:** Customer Inactive = Yes.
*   **Analytics Question:** Which customers are likely to be Inactive?

**Confusion Matrices (Predicted Class vs Actual Class):**

*   **Model A:**
    *   Actual: Yes | Predicted: Yes = 24 | Predicted: No = 16
    *   Actual: No | Predicted: Yes = 3 | Predicted: No = 57
*   **Model B:**
    *   Actual: Yes | Predicted: Yes = 36 | Predicted: No = 4
    *   Actual: No | Predicted: Yes = 18 | Predicted: No = 42
*   **Model C:**
    *   Actual: Yes | Predicted: Yes = 32 | Predicted: No = 8
    *   Actual: No | Predicted: Yes = 7 | Predicted: No = 53

**Required Tasks:**

1.  **Calculate Metrics for all Models:** Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F-measure (F1-score). Show formulas, substitutions, and final values.
2.  **Select Primary Metric:** Justify based on the impact of False Positive and False Negative errors on the Retention Decision.
3.  **Select Candidate Model:** Use the Primary Metric selected in Step 2 to support the choice.

**Note:** Answers may differ; however, the chosen Metric must logically justify the selected Model.
