**Summary of Slide 32 (lect07): Final Test Evidence Estimates Generalization**

The slide presents a structured framework for understanding how final test evidence supports generalization in data science models, while clearly delineating its limitations.

**Most Important Fact:**
Final Test Evidence provides an estimate of Generalization but does NOT guarantee future Performance.

**Key Points (in descending order of importance):**

1. **What the Evidence Supports:**
   - The Final Test Set estimates performance on unseen customers with characteristics similar to those in the test sample.
   - It validates that modeling decisions and metric meanings are consistent during development.

2. **Process Flow:**
   - Before the Final Test: Modeling Decisions, Metric/Decision Meaning, and Final Test Set are established — all unused in prior development.
   - Use Protected Evidence Once: A Selected Customer Tree is evaluated on the Final Test Set.
   - What the Evidence Supports: Performance estimation for similar unseen customers.

3. **What Limits the Conclusion:**
   - Sample Quality and Representativeness affect validity.
   - Leakage Risk and Uncertainty constrain confidence in extrapolation.

4. **Claim Boundary:**
   - Final Test Evidence serves as foundational knowledge for this study only.
   - It explicitly does not serve as a warranty or guarantee of future performance under different conditions or populations.

5. **Takeaway:**
   - The Test Set gives an estimate of Generalization — it is not a promise about future outcomes.
