**Weighted Gini Compares Candidate Splits**

The slide illustrates how to select the best split for a decision tree using **Weighted Gini**.

*   **Parent Node:**
    *   Contains 6 Active and 6 Inactive observations.
    *   **Gini(parent) = 0.500**

*   **Candidate A:**
    *   Condition: `days_since_last_purchase <= 45`
    *   Left Child: 5 Active, 1 Inactive
    *   Right Child: 1 Active, 5 Inactive
    *   **Weighted Gini(A) = 0.278**
    *   **Gini Gain(A) = 0.222**

*   **Candidate B:**
    *   Condition: `purchases_last_90_days <= 2`
    *   Left Child: 2 Active, 4 Inactive
    *   Right Child: 4 Active, 2 Inactive
    *   **Weighted Gini(B) = 0.444**
    *   **Gini Gain(B) = 0.056**

*   **Preferred Candidate: A**
    *   Reason: Has lower Weighted child impurity and higher Impurity reduction compared to Candidate B.

*   **Takeaway:**
    *   The best split adjusts both Child Nodes by weighting them according to the number of Observations in each node.
