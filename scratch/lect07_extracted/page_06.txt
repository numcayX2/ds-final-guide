**Prediction-Time Features and the Future Target**

**Key Concept:**
*   **Prediction-Time Features:** Must exist at the time of prediction.
*   **Target:** Records what happens after the observation date.
*   **Observation Date:** The specific date for which a prediction is being made for each customer.

**Correct Approach (Before Observation Date):**
*   Use historical data available prior to the observation date as features.
*   Examples: `days_since_last_purchase`, `purchases_last_90_days`.

**Incorrect Approach (Data Leakage):**
*   Using future information (e.g., `purchases_next_60_days`) as a feature causes data leakage.
*   This occurs when the target variable (`Inactive = Yes / No`) is determined using data from the "Future Outcome Window" (60 days after the observation date), which is not available at prediction time.

**Takeaway:**
*   Features used in prediction must be known at the time of making the prediction.
