The most important outcome is that a Model must support the decision to retain customers, not just create a Class Label.

**The Customer Inactivity Decision**
*   Prediction has value when it helps answer clear questions and supports sound decision-making.

**Process Flow:**
1.  **Decision Question**: Which customers are at risk of becoming Inactive?
2.  **Prediction**: Determines if a customer is Inactive = Yes / No.
3.  **Retention Decision**: Which customers should be prioritized for retention when the team can only contact some?
