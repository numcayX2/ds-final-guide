Evaluation of classification models begins with comparing Actual Classes ($y\_true$) and Predicted Classes ($y\_pred$) for each observation.

**Row-level Evidence (Customer A–D):**
*   **Agreements:** Customer A and B match the model’s predictions, indicated by green checkmarks and the Thai word "ตรงกัน" (Correct).
*   **Disagreements:** Customer C and D do not match, marked by red crosses and the Thai phrase "ไม่ตรงกัน" (Incorrect).

**From Rows to Metrics:**
Metrics summarize these agreements and disagreements across all customers to produce an evaluation score.

**Takeaway:** Every Evaluation Score originates from the Actual and Predicted Outcomes of each individual Observation.
