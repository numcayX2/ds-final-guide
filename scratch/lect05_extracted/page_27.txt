**Build an Honest Story from Evidence**

The slide presents a framework for constructing data narratives that are honest and evidence-based. It emphasizes that a Data Story should not simply add language to embellish charts but should instead tell a compelling story grounded in facts, addressing key questions about what the data shows, why it matters, and what limitations exist.

**Key Components:**
*   **Context**: Defines the audience and business/analytics question being addressed.
*   **Evidence**: Presents the supporting data through charts and graphs.
*   **Insight**: Explains the meaning or conclusion drawn from the evidence.
*   **Limitation**: Acknowledges any uncertainties or constraints in the data or analysis.
*   **Implication**: Outlines the next steps or actions based on the insights derived.

The visual elements include icons representing people (audience), bar charts, line graphs, magnifying glasses (analysis), warning signs (limitations), and branching arrows (implications). The takeaway reinforces that a good Data Story honestly communicates significance, supported by evidence, while acknowledging its own boundaries.
