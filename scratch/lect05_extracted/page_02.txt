**Hero: The Complete CRISP-DS Workflow**

The slide outlines the **CRISP-DS Workflow**, which is an iterative process involving six key phases:
1.  **Business Understanding**
2.  **Data Understanding**
3.  **Data Preparation**
4.  **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
5.  **Modeling**
6.  **Evaluation**
7.  **Deployment & Communication**
8.  **AI-assisted Improvement**

An arrow indicates that the workflow can loop back from **AI-assisted Improvement** to **Business Understanding**.

A specific note highlights that **Initial EDA** can begin during **Data Understanding** and continue into **Data Preparation**.

---

**Key Takeaways:**

*   **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** connects prepared data to subsequent stages of the Data Science Workflow.
*   It serves as a bridge between data preparation and further analysis steps.
