**Summary of Slide 4: “Begin EDA with an Analytical Question”**

The slide emphasizes that Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) should start with a clear **Analytical Question**, not with visualizations. It illustrates this through a sequential workflow and contrasts it with common pitfalls.

1. **Start EDA with an Analytical Question**  
   The core principle is to begin by defining the analytical question — which determines what variables, summaries, comparisons, and visualizations are relevant. This ensures purpose-driven analysis.

2. **Workflow from Analytical Question → Variables → Summary & Comparison → Visualization**

   - Perform **summary and comparison** (shown as table ↔ bar chart).  
   - End with appropriate **visualization** (bar chart + magnifying glass icon), tailored to answer the initial question.

   > *(“Don’t start EDA with graphs; start with questions.”)*
