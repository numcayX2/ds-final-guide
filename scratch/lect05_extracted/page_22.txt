Honest Visual Encoding Protects Meaning

- Visualization that makes comparison clear without exaggerating, hiding, or altering the basis to change meaning.
- Same data can create different understandings; check visual encoding before communicating.
- Before vs After: A bar chart is shown with emphasis on context (title, labels, units), comparison (baseline, scale, ordering), and emphasis (color, annotation).
- Goal: Clarity, no drama.
- Takeaway: Charts should protect standards instead of creating tension.
