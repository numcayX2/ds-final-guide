Exploratory and Explanatory Visualizations Serve Different Purposes.

Data is the same, but objectives differ:
- **Exploratory Visualization**: For the Analyst. Many questions, many perspectives, very detailed. Used to find Candidate Evidence.
- **Explanatory Visualization**: For the Audience. One main message, selected evidence, Context that is necessary. Used to communicate clearly.

The process moves from Explore to Explain: Select → Simplify → Contextualize → Refine.

Takeaway: Explore broadly for yourself, then explain selectively for the Audience.
