The most important fact is that relationships between variables reveal how they move together, specifically illustrated by the positive correlation between Website Activity and Purchase Frequency.

Key points include:
- **Direction**: Variables can move in the same direction or opposite directions.
- **Strength**: This refers to how closely observations cluster around a specific form.
- **Form**: Relationships may take the shape of a straight line, a curve, or have no clear pattern.
- **Unusual Cases**: These are individual data points that do not follow the overall trend.
- **Takeaway**: It is essential to describe how variables move together before proposing reasons why.
