The slide explains that the choice of measure of center (Mean, Median, or Mode) depends on the Data Type, Distribution, and Analytical Question. The Mean uses all data values and is suitable when outliers are not a concern. The Median represents the middle value in a ranked dataset and is preferred when data is skewed or contains outliers. The Mode identifies the most frequent category and is appropriate for categorical data where the focus is on common occurrences. The takeaway emphasizes selecting the central tendency based on evidence rather than familiarity, using Quantitative Data for Mean, Ranked Order or Numbers for Median, and Categorical for Mode.
