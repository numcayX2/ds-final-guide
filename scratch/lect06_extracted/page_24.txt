### Summary

**Supervised Learning Overview:**
- **Concept:** "Learn to label"
- **Diagram:** A graph with a dashed line representing the model's predictions. The x-axis and y-axis are labeled with icons (a wheel and a cup) and images of limes and apples. Correct predictions are marked with green circles, while incorrect ones are marked with red crosses.
- **Evaluation:**
  - **Definition:** Measuring the model’s quality of prediction against the gold standard (i.e., answers).
  - **Formula:** 
    \[
    \text{Accuracy} = \frac{\text{Correct prediction}}{\text{Data size}}
    \]
  - **Example Calculation:**
    \[
    \text{Accuracy} = \frac{7}{9} = 77.78\%
    \]

**Key Points:**
- The slide introduces supervised learning as a method where the goal is to learn how to label data correctly.
- Evaluation involves comparing the model's predictions to actual labels (gold standard).
- Accuracy is calculated by dividing the number of correct predictions by the total data size.
- In the provided example, 7 out of 9 predictions were correct, resulting in an accuracy of 77.78%.
