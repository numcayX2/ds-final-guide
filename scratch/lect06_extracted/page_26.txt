Classification predicts categories, assigning observations to groups and often providing probability estimates. The target variable is categorical.

Key concepts include:
- **Binary Classification**: Distinguishes between two classes (e.g., Active / Inactive).
- **Multiclass Classification**: Distinguishes among three or more classes (e.g., Active / At Risk / Inactive).

The output consists of the Predicted Class plus Estimated Probability.

Takeaway: Classification predicts categories—and categories can be binary or multiclass.
