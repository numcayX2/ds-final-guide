**Slide 9 of lect06: Modeling Techniques**

The single most important fact is that there are two main categories of modeling techniques in data science.

1.  **Descriptive/Unsupervised Modeling:** This type involves finding relationships (Association) or grouping data (Clustering), without a specific prediction goal.
    *   Description (เชิงสถิติ)
    *   Association
    *   Clustering

2.  **Predictive/Supervised Modeling:** This type uses inference from current data to predict future outcomes.
    *   Classification
    *   Estimation
    *   Prediction
