**Slide 15: Supervised Learning**

- **Heading**: "1. Supervised Learning"
- **Key Concept**: “Learn to label”
- **Step 1: Training**
  - Example of labeled data: An apple is shown with a green leaf.
  - Process: The labeled example (apple) is fed into a model represented by a robot-like figure.
  - Outcome: The model learns criteria for classification based on the labeled examples.

- **Model Details**:
  - The model uses specific features like shape and color to classify objects.
  - Two categories are presented: "What is apple?" and "What is lime?".
    - For "What is apple?": Shape and color are used as criteria.
    - For "What is lime?": Similarly, shape and color are considered.

- **Learning Objective**:
  - The primary goal is to learn how to classify objects from labeled examples.
  - Example given: Classifying apples from limes.
