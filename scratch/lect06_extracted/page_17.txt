**1. Supervised Learning**

*   **“Learn to label”**
*   **Learning how to classify objects from labeled examples**
*   **Model: criteria of classification**
*   **Ex: classifying apples from limes**

**Step 1: Training**

*   **Input:** A labeled example image of an **apple**.
*   **Process:** The system (depicted by a robot) analyzes the input.
*   **Output (MODEL):** A model that defines classification criteria based on **shape** and **color**.
    *   **What is apple?:** Defined by specific **shape** and **color** criteria.
    *   **What is lime?:** Defined by different **shape** and **color** criteria.
