The intended output defines the modeling question.

A single dataset of E-commerce customer behavior can support many different tasks because the question defines the output.

Specific examples include:
*   **Classification or probability:** "Will the customer stop buying within 60 days?" (Output type)
*   **Regression/Forecasting:** "How much will the customer spend next month?" (Output value: $12,450)
*   **Clustering:** "Do customers have similar behaviors?" (Grouping by similarity)
