**Personas เชื่อม Profile เข้ากับบริบทการตัดสินใจ**

*   **Definition:** A Persona is a data-driven observation of behavior, not a claim about the type of person.
*   **Observation-to-Persona Mapping:**
    *   High purchase frequency + high spending $\rightarrow$ **Loyal customers**
    *   Low Engagement $\rightarrow$ **Disengaging customers**
    *   Price-sensitive patterns $\rightarrow$ **Bargain-focused customers**
*   **Guideline:** Names should be based on evidence, not replace it.
