**Step 2:** Find the distance between data points, i.e., find the distance from the data.
*   **Data Point A:** $A = (x1, x2)$
*   **Centroid Y:** $Y = (y1, y2)$

**Solution Formula:**
$$Distance_{manhattan}(A, Y) = |x1 - y1| + |x2 - y2| + ... + |xn - yn|$$
