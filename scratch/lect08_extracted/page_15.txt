**Slide 15: Distance Function (การวัดระยะห่าง)**

*   **Definition**: Defines a distance function between two multivariate data points, $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$.
*   **Euclidean Distance Formula**:
    $$d_{\text{Euclidean}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \sqrt{\sum_i (x_i - y_i)^2}$$
*   **Manhattan or City-block Distance Formula**:
    $$d_{\text{Manhattan}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \sum_i |x_i - y_i|$$
*   **Minkowski Distance Formula**: Presented as the general case of both Euclidean and Manhattan distances.
    $$d_{\text{Minkowski}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \left( \sum_i |x_i - y_i|^p \right)^{1/p}$$
