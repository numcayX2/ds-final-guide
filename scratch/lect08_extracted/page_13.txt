Euclidean Distance measures similarity using straight-line distance.

Data points with smaller Euclidean distances are considered more similar.

The formula for calculating straight-line distance using Euclidean Distance (L2) is:
d(p, q) = √Σᵢ(pᵢ - qᵢ)²

In the two-dimensional feature space diagram:
- Observation 1 is point p = (x₁, y₁).
- Observation 2 is point q = (x₂, y₂).
- The x-axis represents Feature 1 (x₁).
- The y-axis represents Feature 2 (x₂).
- The dashed line between them represents the Euclidean Distance.
