**Slide 32: K-Means Learning with Centroid Stabilization**

**Core Concept:**
K-Means alternates between **Assign** and **Update** steps until the **Centroid** is almost stationary.

**The Iterative Process:**
1.  **Assign:** Assign points to the group of the nearest Centroid.
2.  **Update:** Calculate new values (centroid).
3.  **Check movement:** Determine how much the Centroid has changed.

**Convergence Definition:**
*   Defined as "the position that stabilizes after setting a value."
*   **Crucial Note:** Convergence does not prove that the group is useful.

**Stabilization Condition:**
*   If there is very little change, the **Centroids stabilize**.
*   If there is still enough change, the process continues ("ให้ทำซ้ำต่อ").
