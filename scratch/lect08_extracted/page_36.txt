### Slide 36: Internal Evaluation (ใช้ข้อมูลภายใน cluster เอง)

#### Key Concepts and Headings:
- **Internal Evaluation**: This section focuses on evaluating the quality of clustering using internal data without relying on external labels.
- **Compactness**: Refers to how closely related points are within a cluster.
- **Separation**: Indicates how distinct or clearly separated different clusters are from each other.

#### Formulas:
- **Silhouette Score Formula**:
  \[
  s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max(a(i), b(i))}
  \]
  Where:
  - \(a(i)\): Average distance between point \(i\) and all other points in its own cluster (intra-cluster similarity).
  - \(b(i)\): Nearest cluster mean distance (inter-cluster similarity).

#### Detailed Explanation:
- **Intra-cluster Similarity (\(a(i)\))**: Measures the compactness of a cluster by calculating the average distance between a point and all other points within the same cluster.
- **Inter-cluster Similarity (\(b(i)\))**: Evaluates the separation of a cluster by determining the nearest cluster mean distance, indicating how distinct the cluster is from others.

#### Numerical Example:
- For a well-separated and compact cluster:
  - \(a(i)\) should be small (indicating high intra-cluster similarity).
  - \(b(i)\) should be large (indicating high inter-cluster similarity).

#### Takeaways:
- **Silhouette Score Range**: The score ranges from -1 to 1. A value close to 1 indicates good clustering, while a value close to -1 suggests poor clustering.
- **Advantages**: Does not require true labels, making it useful for unsupervised learning scenarios.
- **Disadvantages**: Can be biased if datasets have significantly different cluster sizes.
