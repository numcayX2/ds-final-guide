Good clustering is defined by two core principles: maximizing Intra-Cluster Similarity and minimizing Inter-Cluster Similarity.

Key points:
• The goal is to group items such that those within the same cluster are as similar as possible (Maximize Intra-Cluster Similarity).
• Simultaneously, different clusters should be as dissimilar as possible from each other (Minimize Inter-Cluster Similarity).

Visual representation on slide 10 of lect08 illustrates this with three distinct groups of data points in a 3D space:
• One group contains pink octagons; labeled “Intra-cluster similarity are maximized.”
• Two other groups contain blue and orange octagons respectively; labeled “Inter-cluster similarity are minimized,” indicating separation between these groups.
